## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

119 days.

That is the median entry-to-issue wait for a Commercial Tenant Finish Out permit among permits issued in Nashville in the last three years - the permit a business needs before it can fit out and open. The median for all issued permits is 29 days, and simple classes like Tree Removal issue at a median of 0 days in the same layer.

The figures are among issued permits only: Metro's Building Permits Issued layer (29,651 permits, pulled November 3) contains no pending or never-issued applications, and the brief prints that limit with every figure. Permits in the window carry $13.23B of applicant-stated construction value; District 19 holds 23.8% of it, at a 62-day median wait.
